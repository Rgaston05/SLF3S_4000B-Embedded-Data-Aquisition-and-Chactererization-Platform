Today I will be designing a controlled, gravity-fed reservoir experiment that allows me to measure how reservoir elevation 
affects water flow through a tube 

Equipment used: 
1. Adjustable reservoir: to provide water at different elevations
2. Tubing: to simulate offshore discharge line, eventually representing the 20-meter horizontal distance
3. Sensirion SLF3S-4000B: to measure volumetric flow rate through the tubing
4. Computer(hydrolab): to run Sensirion's ControlCenter software to acquire and record sensor readings
5. Collection container: to capture discharged water so I can independently verify flow measurements
6. Measuring instruments: A tape measure, a graduated container, and a stopwatch
